Deterministic Dynamic Allocation Algorithm

How the Flask backend deterministically solves resource allocation under carrier constraints

01. Detect Demand

Traffic Monitoring

Orchestrator continuously monitors requested bandwidth demands (Mbps) and computes total demand.

02. Reserve Baselines

Prevent Starvation

Guarantees minimum slice reservations (15 Mbps Emergency, 10 Mbps IoT, 5 Mbps Video). Total 30 Mbps protected.

03. Priority Scheduling

Dynamic Grant

Remaining 70 Mbps capacity pool is granted strictly in order of service priority: Emergency first, then IoT, then Video.

04. Invariant Assertion

Constraint Check

Backend validates SUM(allocations) == 100 Mbps strictly and reports any unmet demand when requested > 100 Mbps.
